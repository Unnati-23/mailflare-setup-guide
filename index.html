CUSTOM DOMAIN EMAIL

Set up your own @yourbrand.com email

Mailflare is an open-source GitHub repo that gives you an address like hello@yourbrand.com. You deploy it on your own Cloudflare account, connect your domain and create your first mailbox. It also gives you a calendar, a booking link and your own file drive.

Source: Get Mailflare on GitHub →
github.com/hieunc229/mailflare

Before you start

What you need

  • A Cloudflare account. Sign up at dash.cloudflare.com.
  • A domain you own (like yourbrand.com) that uses Cloudflare DNS. If you bought it on GoDaddy, Namecheap or Hostinger, Step 0 shows how to move its DNS to Cloudflare. It stays registered where it is.
  • A way to send email: a Resend account, or Cloudflare's own Email Sending (Step 5).
  • About 20–30 minutes. You won't need to write any code.

Step-by-step

How to set it up

Do these steps in order. Every one of them is done in your web browser.

STEP 0 · ONLY IF YOUR DOMAIN ISN'T ON CLOUDFLARE YET

Move your domain's DNS to Cloudflare

  1. In Cloudflare, click Add a domain (also called "Add site"), type your domain and pick a plan.
  2. Cloudflare shows you two nameservers. Copy them.
  3. Log in where you bought the domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap and so on), find Nameservers and replace the existing ones with Cloudflare's two.
  4. Wait until Cloudflare says the domain is Active. This often takes minutes but can take a few hours.
Important: use the same Cloudflare account for your domain and for Mailflare.

STEP 1

Create your Cloudflare API token (CF_TOKEN)

Mailflare uses this token to set up email for your domain. You need it before you deploy.

  1. In the Cloudflare dashboard go to Manage Account → Account API Tokens → Create Token and choose a custom token.
  2. Add a policy for your domain (choose Specified Domains and pick yours) with these permissions:

Policy 1 · Your domain(s)

  • DNS & Zones → DNS → Edit
  • DNS & Zones → Zone → Read
  • DNS & Zones → Zone Settings → Edit
  • Email & Messaging → Email Routing Rules → Edit

Policy 2 · Entire account (only if Cloudflare will send your mail)

  • Email & Messaging → Email Sending → Edit
  1. Create the token and copy the secret right away. Cloudflare only shows it once. Paste it into your notes for now.
Common mistake: paste only the token's secret. Don't use the token ID, and don't put the word Bearer in front of it.

STEP 2

Deploy Mailflare to Cloudflare

This is the one-click part.

DEPLOY TO CLOUDFLARE →

  1. Click the button above and sign in to Cloudflare.
  2. Choose the Cloudflare account that owns your domain.
  3. Keep the app name exactly mailflare. Don't rename it, or receiving email will break.
  4. When it asks for variables or secrets, add CF_TOKEN and paste the token from Step 1.
  5. Start the deployment and wait for Cloudflare to finish. It creates the database, storage and queues for you.
Prefer an AI agent? There's a copy-paste prompt for Claude Code or Cursor further down.

STEP 3

Create your admin account

  1. Open the URL of your new mailflare app. Cloudflare shows it when deployment finishes, and it looks like mailflare.your-name.workers.dev.
  2. If you don't land on the setup page automatically, add /setup to the end of the URL.
  3. Mailflare checks your Cloudflare settings and prepares the database for you.
  4. Create your admin account with a strong password. This is the login you'll use from now on.

STEP 4

Connect your domain & create your first mailbox

  1. Type your domain (for example yourbrand.com).
  2. Pick Cloudflare as the service that receives mail. Mailflare turns on Email Routing and adds the DNS records itself.
  3. Choose your first address, for example hello@yourbrand.com, and finish.
  4. Send a test email to it from your personal Gmail. It should show up in your Mailflare inbox.
Already have email on this domain? If Google, Zoho or another provider already handles mail for this domain, Mailflare will ask before it replaces their MX records. Once you confirm, your old provider stops receiving mail for that domain.

STEP 5

Turn on sending

Receiving works now. To send and reply from your new address, pick one option:

Resend

  1. Sign up at resend.com and create a full-access API key.
  2. In Mailflare, open Admin → Domains and expand your domain.
  3. Under Setup sending email, switch on Resend, paste the key and click Setup.
  4. Wait a few minutes, then click Check status until it's verified.

Cloudflare

  1. Go to Compute → Email Service → Email Sending, click Onboard domain and follow the prompts.
  2. Make sure your token has the Email Sending permission from Step 1.
  3. In Mailflare, open Admin → Domains, expand your domain and set up DKIM & DMARC.
Test it: reply to the test email from Step 4. If it reaches your Gmail, you're done. 🎉

Now use it

Everything else you get

More email addresses

Create as many addresses as you need (support@, sales@, your own name@) from the admin area. Each one can be personal or shared with your team, and they all show up in one inbox. You can also set routing rules to store, forward, reject or sort incoming mail. To add another domain later, open Admin → Domains → New domain.

Calendar

Create events, including repeating ones, with proper time zones. Anyone you add as an attendee gets an email invitation from your own domain.

Booking link

Share one public link (in your Instagram bio, email signature or DMs) so people can book an open slot on your calendar.

Drive

Upload files and share them with clients. Everything is stored in your own Cloudflare storage.

AI email assistant

Open Assistant → Settings for a mailbox and turn it on. It can search your mail, draft replies and manage events. Drafts wait for you to review them, so nothing is sent without your OK.

Moving from Gmail?

Mailflare can import old mail over IMAP, from a Gmail Takeout export, or from .eml and .mbox files.

Stuck?

FAQ & fixes

Error 9109: "Invalid access token"

The Deploy button's own token is not the same thing as CF_TOKEN. You need the separate token from Step 1. Check that:

  • You pasted the token secret, not the token ID.
  • There's no Bearer in front of it.
  • You redeployed after changing any variables or secrets.
Error 2008 or "MX records for another mail service"

Your domain already points its mail at another provider. Only one service can receive mail for a domain. Mailflare asks before it replaces those records, and once you confirm, the old provider stops getting mail. Your token needs the DNS → Edit permission to do this.

Error 10000 on Email Routing

Your token is missing permissions. Go back to Step 1 and make sure it has DNS Edit, Zone Read, Zone Settings Edit and Email Routing Rules Edit for your domain.

Emails aren't arriving
  • The app is named exactly mailflare in Cloudflare.
  • Email Routing is turned on for your domain in Cloudflare.
  • The mailbox has an Email Routing rule that points to the mailflare Worker.
  • Your domain and Mailflare are on the same Cloudflare account.
My domain is on GoDaddy / Namecheap. Will it work?

Yes. Keep the domain where you bought it and just switch its nameservers to Cloudflare (Step 0). The domain stays registered where it is.
